Privacy & quote form

This website is published as a static GitHub Pages site. It does not maintain its own contact database or login system.

Information you provide

When you submit a quote request, you may provide your name, phone number, email, preferred callback time, job location, requested service, urgency, and job details. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other highly sensitive information.

Form delivery

The quote form uses the third-party service FormSubmit to deliver your request to radixrepair@gmail.com. The provider may process submitted information and apply spam filtering. Review the provider’s privacy information before submitting. You can alternatively call or email us directly.

How we use inquiries

Details are used to review your service request, contact you, and discuss scheduling and pricing. Submitting a request does not create a contract or confirm an appointment.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request that we delete an inquiry from our records, email radixrepair@gmail.com.
